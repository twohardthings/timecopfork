Time Cop Fork is a maintained fork of Time Cop 1.8.1 by Kenton Hamaluik, kept working on current Android versions.

What's different in the fork:
- Importing a database works on Android 13 and newer. The fork uses Android's own file dialog and needs no storage permission. The original app asks for a permission that Android no longer grants and only shows "Storage permission required" (https://github.com/hamaluik/timecop/issues/169).
- Its own app ID, so it installs next to the original app. To move your entries, export the database in the original app and import it in the fork.
- Updated for current Android versions, with a purple icon to tell the two apps apart.

Features:
- Offline-only, mobile-first, for Android
- Fully private—there is no tracking / spying / advertising / etc
- Keep track of tasks with multiple parallel timers that can be started with the tap of a button
- Associate timers with projects to group your work (or don't)
- Start, stop, edit, and delete timers whenever with no fuss
- Export data as a .csv or PDF file, filtered by time-spans and projects
- Export and import the app's database for full access to all of its data
- Automatic light mode / dark mode based on your device settings
- Available in 18 languages, most of them machine-translated
- Open source (licensed under Apache-2.0): https://github.com/twohardthings/timecopfork
